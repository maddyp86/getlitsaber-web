# Litsaber Brand

This document is the strategic spine of the website. Every page, component, and line of copy should pass through it. If a decision doesn't reinforce the thesis below, it doesn't belong on the site.

---

## The Repositioning Thesis

> **Litsaber is a festival accessory that happens to hit 510 carts — not a vape battery with an LED.** Its meant for buyers who self-identify by what's visible at the night, not by what's hidden in their pocket. Every product, copy, design, and channel decision should foreground visibility, lifestyle, and the engineered hardware that makes it credible — and should refuse any framing that competes Litsaber on discretion, commodity vape specs, or low price.

### What this rules out

- "Discreet" or "pocket-sized" framing
- Spec-leading copy that puts mAh / voltage / 510 thread before the experience
- Comparison-shopping against $20 vape pens
- Apologetic "it's also a vape" softening — the hits-510-carts part is matter-of-fact, not the headline

### What this rules in

- Imagery of nightlife, festivals, dark rooms with color
- Copy that names the moment ("the headliner," "the afters," "the walk home")
- Engineering details framed as *credibility for the experience*, not as the product
- Comparisons against glowsticks, lightshow gear, and lifestyle accessories — not other batteries

## Audience

The buyer self-identifies by what's visible at night. Three behavioral archetypes, not demographic personas:

**The Headliner.** Festival regular, raver, EDM listener. Already owns LED gloves, light-up gear, glow accessories. Cares that the device *looks like* it belongs in the experience, not next to it. Discovery channel: TikTok, Instagram, festival activations.

**The Host.** Throws parties, runs the playlist, hands things to friends. Wants something that creates a moment when it comes out of a pocket. Discovery channel: word of mouth, friend's-friend recommendation.

**The Collector.** Buys things because they're well-made, not because they're cheap. Cares about materials, finish, the engineering story. Cross-shops with premium accessories, not vape products. Discovery channel: product detail pages, press, niche communities.

All three convert on **the visible-at-night thesis**. None of them convert on specs alone.

## Voice & Tone

The voice is **observed, specific, confident.** It doesn't sell — it names what's already happening.

### Patterns that work (pulled directly from the Figma copy)

- *"Most batteries hide in your pocket. Litsaber is the thing the stranger across the bar walks over to ask about."*
- *"Lightshow gear at the headliner. The flare you raise when your friend's looking for you in the crowd. The flashlight on the walk back."*
- *"Built for the headliner, the afters, and the next weekend after that."*
- *"Clean hits. Lit clouds."*
- *"It's a glowstick that hits 510 carts, not a vape pen with a small indicator light."*

### What these patterns share

1. **Three-beat rhythm.** Short clauses, often in threes. Mirrors the way moments compound at a night.
2. **Named moments, not adjectives.** "The headliner" beats "exciting." "The walk back" beats "convenient."
3. **Comparison frames.** "X, not Y" is a recurring move. It defines the product by what it isn't.
4. **No exclamation marks. No hype words.** "Amazing," "revolutionary," "premium" — none of these appear. The product talks like someone confident enough not to oversell.

### Voice rules

- **Do** name specific moments (headliner, afters, walk home, crowd, bar)
- **Do** use three-beat rhythm in lists and CTAs
- **Do** lead with what's visible, follow with what's engineered
- **Do** treat 510-thread compatibility as a feature, not a category
- **Don't** lead with mAh, voltage, or thread spec
- **Don't** use "premium," "revolutionary," "best-in-class," or any superlative
- **Don't** apologize for or hide the cannabis context (FAQ handles it directly)
- **Don't** use em-dashes in body copy (per house style)
- **Don't** use exclamation marks
- **Don't** stack adjectives — pick one strong noun instead

## Pricing & Commercial Rules

- **MSRP is $59.99.** Across all DTC, marketing, and outreach. (Note: this updates an earlier internal rule of $60. $59.99 is the locked price moving forward.)
- **Wholesale starts at MOQ 25 at $24/unit.** Free display case and demo unit at 100+ units. Full tier table lives in the wholesale one-sheet, not on the public site.
- **Pricing is excluded from outreach.** Public site shows MSRP and wholesale entry point only.

## Visual System (Summary)

Full tokens live in `tokens.json`. The directional read:

- **Pure black backgrounds.** Cyan is the brand color. White is the primary text. Magenta is a sparing secondary accent.
- **Glow effects** on hero headlines and key callouts — white glow on primary, cyan glow on supporting.
- **Five-font system:** Stellar (display headlines), Monoton (single-word accents), Orbitron (sub-headings), Inter (body), Space Mono (UI labels and CTAs).
- **Spec pills** with deep-purple borders and cyan text — used to surface hardware credibility without leading with specs.
